# LinkedIn profile — example

Copy this to `~/.claude/profiles/linkedin.md` and replace every value with your
own. Everything here is invented.

Both `linkedin-post-writer` and `linkedin-comment-writer` read this one file.

---

## Who I am

- **Name:** Jane Dela Cruz
- **Based in:** Davao City, Philippines
- **Role:** Senior web developer, 7 years in WordPress
- **Field:** websites for medical and professional practices

## What I work with

WordPress, Laravel, Next.js (headless WP), ACF, Elementor, caching and
performance tooling, managed hosting, form and email plugins, Figma-to-website,
a task tracker, AI coding assistants, workflow automation.

Day to day: building and maintaining client sites, SEO, blog content, monthly
performance and security checks.

## Off-limits

Things that must never appear in a post or comment:

- Client names, employer name, project names
- Any domain name or URL, including my own
- Specific tool or AI product names (describe them generally instead)
- Rates, invoice amounts, or anything about a client's contract

## Tone notes

- Plain, simple English. Short sentences.
- First person, like talking to a colleague.
- No buzzwords, no em dashes, at most one emoji.
